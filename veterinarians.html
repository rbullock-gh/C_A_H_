The doctors who will see your pet

All three of our veterinarians trained at the Mississippi State University College of Veterinary Medicine, and between them they have spent most of their careers in Columbia.

Dr. Sanford is a 1985 graduate of the Mississippi State University College of Veterinary Medicine, and he joined Columbia Animal Hospital the same year he qualified. Four decades later, he is still here.

He is married to Wanda Sanford, and they are parents to Elizabeth, William and Jonathan.
