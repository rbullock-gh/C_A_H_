Before you call

What helps us help you faster

  • 1 Your pet's name and rough age If you have been here before, that is usually enough for us to find their record.
  • 2 What you are seeing, and since when Limping since Tuesday, off their food for two days, scratching an ear raw — specifics help us judge urgency.
  • 3 Anything they may have eaten or got into Medications, plants, antifreeze, a bone, a toy. Bring packaging if you have it.
  • 4 For boarding, your dates and vaccination status If a booster is due, call four weeks before the stay so there is time to sort it.
