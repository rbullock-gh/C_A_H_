# Content sources

Every factual claim on this site traces to a row below. Nothing here was invented.

The existing site, `columbiaanimalhospital.net`, is **blocked by this environment's network
egress policy** — `WebFetch` returns `EGRESS_BLOCKED` and `curl` gets `403 CONNECT`. So did
every mirror and archive route tried (`r.jina.ai`, `web.archive.org`, Yelp, GeniusVets,
Pawlicy, the Marion County directory). All source text below was therefore recovered through
**search-engine extracts** of those pages, cross-checked across differently-worded queries.

That matters for one thing in particular: **near-verbatim is not verbatim.** Sentences
reproduced identically across two or more independent queries are marked HIGH; anything
recovered once is marked MEDIUM and was either rewritten in our own words or left out.

> A name collision polluted almost every search. There are at least six unrelated practices
> called "Columbia Animal Hospital" — in Maryland, Missouri, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, and
> a Fear Free clinic at `columbiaanimalhospital**.com**`. Content from those practices
> (notably "Fear Free", "Dr. Flaum", and the answering service `(877) 570-6272`) was found in
> results and **deliberately discarded**. None of it appears on this site.

## Needs confirmation

Ordered by how much damage getting it wrong would do. The first is a launch blocker.

1. **Dr. Wallace Carson.** A bio page for him is live and indexed at
   `/AboutUs/DrWallaceCarson.aspx` — MSU undergraduate, DVM from Auburn University College
   of Veterinary Medicine in 1974, with the practice since 1975. The client brief lists only
   Loper, Sanford and Williams. No obituary and no retirement announcement could be found;
   neither could any positive evidence that he is currently practising beyond the live page
   and stale directory entries. **He is not published on this site.** Confirm his status
   before adding him — and if he has retired, he may still deserve a line in the history.
2. **How a client actually reaches the on-call vet after hours.** The practice publishes
   *that* an on-call veterinarian exists but **no mechanism for reaching one** — no separate
   number, no answering service, no instruction. This site tells people to call
   (601) 736-3041, the only published number. Confirm what happens when that number is
   called outside hours and reword if needed. This is the single most useful content gap to
   close.
3. **Saturday opening time.** The official site, Facebook and three directories say 8:00 AM.
   Pawlicy and GeniusVets say 7:30 AM. 8:00 AM is used here. If 8:00 is right, correct those
   two listings; a wrong opening time costs walk-ins.
4. **Address formatting.** The site writes `1409 Hwy 98E` — no space before the "E" — and
   that form is used here for consistency with the current site and the brief. Six different
   renderings are live across the web (`Highway 98 E`, `Hwy 98 E`, `HIGHWAY 98 E`,
   `Hwy 98 East`). Two directories carry outright errors: the Marion County Development
   Partnership lists **1498** Hwy 98 East, and US Companies lists **Old** Hwy 98 E. Pick one
   canonical form, match the Google Business Profile to it exactly, and correct the rest.
5. **Microchip brand.** One source reports the practice uses **Avid** microchips; another
   recovered only the generic description. Not published here. Confirm before naming a brand.
6. **Online pharmacy.** One source found a live store at
   `columbiaanimalhospital.myvetstoreonline.pharmacy`; another found no pharmacy link for
   this practice and warned that a similar URL belongs to a different business. **Not linked
   here.** Confirm the correct URL before adding it.
7. **Reproductive services detail.** A list — progesterone evaluation, artificial
   insemination, pregnancy monitoring, assisted birth — surfaced once, alongside a competitor
   site running the same CMS template. Only the single sourced sentence is used here.
8. **Payment methods.** CareCredit acceptance and "all major credit cards" appear only on
   third-party listings, never on the practice's own site. Not published here.
9. **Grooming service area.** Recovered variously as "the Columbia and Hattiesburg area" and
   "the Columbia and Mississippi area". Neither is quoted; the copy says the groomers have
   served the Columbia area for many years, which all sources support.
10. **Appointment vs. walk-in policy.** Only a third-party aggregator claims walk-ins are
    accepted. The site publishes no policy. Not published here.
