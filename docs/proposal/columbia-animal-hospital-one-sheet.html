Website proposal

A website that turns
searches into appointments

A new site for Columbia Animal Hospital, built around one job: when someone in Columbia or Foxworth needs a veterinarian — usually on a phone, in a hurry — they find you, they trust you, and they call.

Built and ready to launch

TodayWhere the current site loses people

  • It was not laid out for a phone. Most people looking for a veterinarian are on one, and pinching and dragging sideways to read your hours is friction at the worst possible moment.
  • Two directories have your address wrong. The Marion County Development Partnership lists 1498 Hwy 98 East. US Companies lists Old Hwy 98 E. Local search ranking rests on every listing reading the same way.
  • Three places say you open at 7:30 on Saturday. You say 8:00. Someone who drives over at 7:30 and finds a locked door does not come back a second time.
  • The whole site exists twice. Every page is also reachable at columbiaanimalhospital.vetsourcecms.com, so search engines split the credit between two copies instead of giving it all to your own domain.

Already builtNineteen pages, finished, waiting on you

Home, About, Services, Our Team, New Patients, Resources, Contact, an appointment request, and a page of its own for each of your nine services.

  • Written from your own published words. Every fact — your hours, your address, what each service involves, what the doctors studied and when — traces back to something the practice already publishes. Where a source could not be confirmed, the claim was left off rather than guessed at.
  • Built to be found. Structured data on every page carrying your hours and the area you serve, one source of truth for the address so it cannot drift, and a migration plan that redirects every old page so nothing you have built up in search is lost. Plain HTML, so there is no page builder and no monthly platform fee underneath it.

Nothing on this site was invented.

No testimonials, no star ratings, no awards, no claim you have not made yourself. The record of where every fact came from — including the ones deliberately left off — is handed over with the site.
