Before you call

What helps us find the right slot

  • 1 Your pet's name and rough age If you have been here before, that is usually enough for us to find their record.
  • 2 What you are seeing, and since when Limping since Tuesday, off their food for two days, scratching an ear raw — specifics help us judge urgency.
  • 3 Anything they may have eaten or got into Medications, plants, antifreeze, a bone, a toy. Bring the packaging if you have it.
  • 4 For grooming or boarding, your dates Both book up quickly. For boarding, call four weeks ahead if a vaccination booster is due.

What you can book

Appointments, grooming and boarding

All three go through the same number. Say which one you are calling about and we will put you through.

A veterinary appointment

Wellness visits, vaccinations, a problem you have noticed, dental work, surgery, or a second opinion.

Grooming

Our groomers are very popular and can often book up quickly, so call well ahead of the date you want.

Boarding

We recommend pets be current on vaccinations before a stay. If a booster is due, contact us four weeks in advance.

Urgent situations

If your pet cannot wait for an appointment

Call the hospital rather than booking the next open slot, and say what is happening. During our posted hours we will tell you whether to come straight in.

We are not a 24-hour emergency hospital. Outside our hours an on-call veterinarian is available for emergencies. Call first and follow the instructions you are given — please do not assume someone is at the building.
