Most clinics and shelters

Microchips are a permanent form of identification. Each microchip contains a code which is unique to your pet.

How it helps when a pet goes missing

In the event your furry friend is lost, they can be taken to most veterinary clinics and animal shelters, where they can be scanned and identified as your pet. A collar and tag can be lost, removed or worn illegible; a chip cannot.

Having it done

Microchipping is quick and is often done during a routine visit — many owners have it done at the same time as a spay or neuter, while the pet is already here. Call the hospital and we will fit it into your next appointment.

Keep your details current

A microchip only works if the contact details attached to it are up to date. If you move house or change your phone number, update the registration.
