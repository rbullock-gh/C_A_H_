Keeping your pet's hair cut, cleaned and brushed is important — it prevents matting and helps keep them cool and clean, which in a Mississippi summer is not a small thing.

What our groomers do

Our services run from trimming and cleaning through to medicated bathing for pets with skin conditions. Because the grooming room is inside a veterinary hospital, a pet whose skin is being treated can be bathed by people who already have their notes.

Experienced hands

Our experienced groomers have been keeping pets in the Columbia area looking and feeling their best for many years. Nervous dogs, matted coats and cats who have opinions about water are all familiar territory.

Book ahead

The groomers at Columbia Animal Hospital are very popular and can often book up quickly. If you have a date in mind — before a holiday, or before the weather turns warm — it is worth calling well in advance.
