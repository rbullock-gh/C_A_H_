The doctors at Columbia Animal Hospital can assist you and your pet through all phases of artificial insemination, right through to the birthing process.

Working to a timetable

Breeding is one of the few areas of veterinary medicine that runs on a calendar rather than on symptoms. Getting the timing right requires planning ahead, so call early rather than when something is already under way.

Talk to us first

Every case is different, and what is appropriate depends on the animal, the breed and where you are in the cycle. Call the hospital and ask for a conversation with one of the doctors before you make arrangements — it is the quickest way to find out what is and is not possible here.
