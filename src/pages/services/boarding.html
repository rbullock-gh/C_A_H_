Leaving a pet behind is the part of a trip nobody looks forward to. Our boarding facility is supervised by our veterinarians and kept clean and comfortable, which means that if something does come up while you are away, a doctor is already in the building.

Comfortable in any season

The facility is climate-controlled to keep boarders cosy during winter and cool during summer.

Dogs and cats, kept apart

We walk each canine boarder daily. Feline friends are kept in a separate area, away from barking dogs, to reduce the stress that noise causes cats.

Before your pet's stay

For the safety of your pet and of the others in our care, we recommend that all animals be current on their vaccinations prior to boarding at Columbia Animal Hospital.

If your pet is due for a vaccination booster, or if you are uncertain of their vaccination status, please contact us four weeks in advance of their stay so we can make sure they are properly vaccinated in time.

What to bring

If your pet requires medication or a special dietary food during boarding, please bring it with you. Familiar food is one less change for them to absorb while you are away.

Reserving a place

Boarding is arranged by phone. Holiday periods fill up, so call as early as you can.
