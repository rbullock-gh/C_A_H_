Skin and ear complaints are among the most common reasons pets are brought to us, and among the most frustrating for owners — because the itching is visible long before the cause is.

Finding out what is actually going on

Our dermatology services include skin scrapings, ear cytologies and skin cytologies. These are quick, in-house tests that tell us whether we are dealing with parasites, a bacterial infection, a yeast overgrowth, or something else — which matters, because the treatments are not interchangeable.

Medicated baths

We also offer medicated baths to help manage skin infections and dermatitis. Our grooming team can carry these out, which means a pet with an ongoing skin condition can be bathed by people who already know their history.

When to bring your pet in

Scratching that does not settle, head-shaking, a smell from the ears, redness between the toes, or bald patches are all worth a look. Skin conditions tend to get more entrenched — and more expensive — the longer they run.
