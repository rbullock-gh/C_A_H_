Our story

Established in 1975, and changed a good deal since

Columbia Animal Hospital was established in 1975. In the decades since, the hospital has undergone significant changes to better serve clients and their pets — new equipment, new doctors, and a steadily narrowing focus.

The largest of those changes came in 2005. We began as a mixed animal practice, looking after livestock and family pets alike, as most rural Mississippi practices did. In 2005 we became a small animal clinic exclusively, so that our training, our equipment and our attention could go entirely to dogs and cats.

It was not a small decision for a rural Mississippi practice. It was the right one. A hospital that sees dogs and cats all day gets better at dogs and cats.

What that means day to day

Care for the whole life of the pet

  • 1 The beginning Puppy and kitten vaccinations at six, nine, twelve and fifteen weeks, the spay-or-neuter conversation, microchipping, and the first heartworm prevention.
  • 2 The long middle Annual visits, dental cleanings, skin and ear problems, the occasional surgery, grooming, and boarding when you travel — all in the same building, with the same records.
  • 3 The later years Senior pets may need blood work to screen for weight-related health problems or the presence of certain cancers. We would rather find those early.
