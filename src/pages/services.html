Routine visits

What a yearly check-up covers

We recommend that a healthy pet visit the clinic at least once each year — to properly boost vaccinations, to identify health issues your pet may be experiencing without obvious signs, and to monitor their aging process.

Puppies and kittens need more than that at the start. They receive some immunity from their mother's milk, but it protects for only a short period, so a series of booster vaccinations three weeks apart is needed — at six, nine, twelve and fifteen weeks. Annual vaccines are then recommended one year after the final booster.
